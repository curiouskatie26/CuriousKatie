What to expect in a session

Sessions take place online, from somewhere comfortable and private. We might start by checking in with how you're feeling, then turn attention toward sensations, emotions, or images as they arise. You're always in charge, and you can pause or stop at any time. No experience is needed.

  1. Arrive and settle

    Join from somewhere comfortable and private, and take a moment to check in with how you're feeling.

  2. Notice and explore

    We turn gentle attention toward sensations, emotions, or images as they arise, with curiosity and no agenda.

  3. Integrate and close

    We take time to let things settle before we finish. You're always in charge, and can pause or stop at any time.
